## Import policy

Drive material can contribute architecture ideas, naming, documentation, source discovery and provenance leads. It cannot bypass v8 gates. Never copy legacy secrets, never load untrusted pickle/joblib artifacts, never convert synthetic/random notebook fields into canonical history, never promote from sample/demo output, and never use automatic wagering execution.

Any candidate evidence recovered from Drive must be separately checksummed, timestamped, provenance-linked and validated under the same chronology, leakage, OOF calibration, Brier/log-loss/ECE, holdout, schema and source-admissibility rules as native PhilthySports evidence.
